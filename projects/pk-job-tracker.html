PK Govt Job Tracker

Scrapes Pakistani government job listings (FPSC, PPSC), extracts real eligibility requirements out of the PDF advertisements they're bundled in via Gemini, and shows every open job grouped by degree level — instead of manually refreshing two government websites.

Live demo View code

scrape → extract eligibility (Gemini) → match

Problem

Government job ads are published as scanned PDFs across multiple sites, so eligibility criteria are effectively unsearchable without manually opening every advertisement.

Approach

A robots.txt-respecting daily scraper pulls listings; a separate, rate-limited PDF backfill step feeds ads through Gemini vision to extract structured eligibility; two independent match signals — a deterministic rule-based match and a semantic-similarity fuzzy score via sentence-transformers — surface relevant jobs.

Result

A Streamlit dashboard with an 8-category degree classification schema (Matric → PhD, plus Professional/Specialist), refreshed daily via GitHub Actions.
