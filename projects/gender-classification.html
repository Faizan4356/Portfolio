Gender Classification

A computer vision project that detects people in an image and classifies them as male or female in real time, built end-to-end from dataset preparation through a live inference frontend.

YOLOv8PyTorchEfficientNetB0TensorFlow/KerasStreamlitOpenCV

predicted vs. actual
Problem

Attaching demographic classification to raw video/image feeds requires two distinct steps — finding people, then classifying each one — that most tutorials skip past.

Approach

Used YOLOv8 to localize people, cropped each detection, and ran it through a fine-tuned EfficientNetB0 classifier, with OpenCV/Pillow gluing the pipeline together into a single Streamlit demo.

Result

A live-inference frontend that draws boxes and gender labels over uploaded images or webcam frames in real time.
