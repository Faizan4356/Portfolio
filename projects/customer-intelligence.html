Customer Intelligence Platform

Started as a churn prediction model and grew into a full Customer Intelligence Platform — combining a production-grade XGBoost churn classifier, a PyTorch LSTM usage-trend model, and a DistilBERT sentiment layer into one 0–100 Health Score, served through a 4-tab Streamlit app.

Live demo View code

AUC 0.839 · optimized for recall on churn class

Problem

A single churn probability doesn't tell a customer success team *why* an account is at risk, or what's changed recently in behavior and sentiment.

Approach

Layered three models — an XGBoost churn classifier trained on real Telco data and optimized for recall, a PyTorch LSTM reading usage-trend sequences, and a pretrained DistilBERT sentiment scorer — then combined them into one interpretable Health Score with SHAP explanations.

Result

A 4-tab Streamlit app (Single Prediction, Search Existing Customer, What-If Simulator, Batch Upload) that's explicit about which parts are real data (churn) vs. synthetic-but-correlated (usage/sentiment).
